Purpose
- Retained as a scratch reference from an older text-extraction note.

Example regex
- `(?s)(?<=SomeText>)(.+?)(?=</SomeText>)`

Meaning
- `(?s)` lets `.` span line breaks.
- `(?<=SomeText>)` starts after the opening marker.
- `(.+?)` captures lazily until the closing marker.
- `(?=</SomeText>)` stops before the closing marker.
